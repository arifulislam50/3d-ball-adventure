Privacy Policy

App: Marblo: Island Roll (package com.arifulislam50.marblo)
Developer: Ariful Islam
Last updated: 23 November 2026

Summary

Marblo is an offline game. It does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, coins, ball skins and settings) is saved only on your own device. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Permissions

  • Vibration – for short vibrations during gameplay. You can turn this off in the game's Settings.
  • Internet – included by the app framework. The game itself does not send or receive any data.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children or anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
